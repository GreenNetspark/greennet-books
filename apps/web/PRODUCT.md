# Product Context

## Product
- Name: GreenNet Books
- Company: GreenNetSpark
- Stage: Production Foundation / Core Phase
- Mode: Operate

## Purpose
Cloud-based accounting, invoicing, taxation, and financial management SaaS tailored specifically for Indian businesses, Chartered Accountants, and finance teams.

## Target Audience
- Small, medium, and large Indian businesses
- Trading, manufacturing, and retail enterprises
- Chartered Accountants and finance controllers
- Service providers and consultants

## Key Capabilities & Scope
- Indian GST Compliance (CGST, SGST, IGST calculations, GSTIN validation, HSN/SAC codes, GSTR preparation)
- Customer & Vendor Master Management
- Separate tracking and allocation of Customer & Vendor Advances (liability & asset treatments)
- Real-time double-entry accounting engine (Journals, General Ledger, Trial Balance, P&L, Balance Sheet)
- Sales and purchase workflows (Estimates, Orders, Invoices, Bills, Credit/Debit Notes)
- Multi-organization and branch management with strict tenant data isolation
- Responsive Web Application (desktop, tablet, mobile)

## Platform
- Primary: Web (Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui)
- Target: Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile Web
- Future: Mobile applications (React Native for Android & iOS)
